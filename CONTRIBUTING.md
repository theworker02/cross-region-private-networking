# Contributing

## Public contributions are not accepted (pre-sale)

This repository is a **proprietary pre-sale** product and documentation package. The Rightsholder is **not** soliciting public pull requests, issues that submit code, or community patches.

### Why

- IP must remain clearly owned for acquisition and commercial licensing (`LICENSE`, `ACQUISITION.md`).  
- Unsolicited contributions can create ownership ambiguity.  
- Granting merge rights must not be mistaken for a grant of **use** rights in the Product.

## If you open a PR or issue anyway

By submitting any patch, suggestion, documentation edit, or idea (“Submission”), you agree that:

1. You have the right to make the Submission.  
2. You **assign** all right, title, and interest in the Submission to **[Rightsholder]**, or, where assignment is ineffective, you grant the Rightsholder a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, modify, commercialize, and relicense the Submission as part of the Product and Materials.  
3. Your Submission does **not** entitle you to use, deploy, or distribute the Product.  
4. The Rightsholder may ignore, reject, delete, or rewrite the Submission without explanation or compensation.  
5. You will not include third-party confidential information or code incompatible with proprietary licensing.

A Contributor License Agreement (CLA) or assignment agreement may be required before any Submission is considered. Until then, **do not expect review**.

## Allowed interactions

Without creating use rights:

- Security reports via [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md)  
- Acquisition / licensing inquiries to `[CONTACT_EMAIL]` (not via public issue trackers for confidential deal terms)
