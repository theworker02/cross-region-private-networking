# Support

## No public support until licensed

This project is **pre-sale and proprietary**. There is **no public support obligation** for unlicensed parties.

| Audience | Support |
|----------|---------|
| General public / unlicensed | None. Do not expect replies on usage, deployment, or implementation help. |
| Parties under NDA evaluation | Diligence Q&A only, as agreed in writing. |
| Commercial licensees | Per the commercial agreement (SLA, channels, hours). |
| Post-acquisition | Per the purchase agreement and transition plan. |

## How to get help (authorized parties)

1. Confirm your agreement is active.  
2. Use the support channel named in that agreement (placeholder: `[SUPPORT_EMAIL]`).  
3. Include environment, region pair (e.g. VA↔FRA), fabric node versions, and non-secret configs.

## What we will not do for unlicensed users

- Debug production deployments of unauthorized copies  
- Provide configuration guidance that enables competitive cloning  
- Bypass `LICENSE` restrictions
