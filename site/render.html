Targeted company: Render

The strategic fit for this product is Render — specifically teams running multi-region services who already rely on Render’s same-region private networking and need a global, identity-aware extension.

Thesis

Prefer Render’s excellent same-region private networking whenever traffic stays in-region (LOCAL_NATIVE). Use Fabric only for cross-region authenticated paths, stable global names (*.global.internal), mTLS identity, and policy — without making every service public by default.

Documented gap (public Render posture)

Render private networks are region-scoped. Cross-region communication commonly uses the public network plus application-level authentication. That is a workable pattern — and also the gap this fabric is designed to narrow for private, named, health-aware service connectivity.

Diligence citations live in the repo gap matrix: docs/RENDER_GAP_MATRIX.md · integration architecture

What this is not

  • Not a Render product, partnership claim, or endorsement
  • Not a claim that live multi-region VA↔FRA was executed in this package (see readiness report)
  • Not a substitute for Render’s same-region private networking — it extends that model
